PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designing a louder
visual signal.

Ganesa FM 88.8 is a community radio station that needed a fresh, flexible visual voice—one that could work as confidently on an on-air announcement as it could in a social post, a poster, or an event banner.

The design direction combines a warm broadcast palette with high-contrast typography and modular forms that stay recognisable across fast-moving content.
